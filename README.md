# UI-UX-Experiment3

## Aim:
To perform a heuristic evaluation of Spotify and YouTube Music by comparing their user experience using Nielsen’s usability heuristics, identifying usability issues, and proposing design improvements to enhance navigation, accessibility, consistency, and overall user satisfaction.

## Algorithm:
1.Select two music streaming applications: Spotify and YouTube Music.
2.Study the interface and features of both applications.
3.Use Nielsen’s 10 Usability Heuristics as the evaluation framework.
4.Analyze important UX factors such as navigation, playlist management, music discovery, error handling, and visual design.
5.Identify usability problems in each application.
6.Assign severity ratings to the identified issues.
7.Compare the strengths and weaknesses of both applications.
8.Propose redesign solutions and UX improvements for each app.
9.Create updated screen mockups or redesign examples.
10.Summarize the findings and provide conclusions based on the evaluation.

Explanation:
Spotify provides a cleaner and more organized user experience compared to YouTube Music. Its interface is simple, consistent, and easy to navigate, especially for playlist management and music discovery. However, Spotify should improve by adding an undo option for deleted songs or playlists, reducing premium popups, and improving recommendation variety and help accessibility.

YouTube Music is strong in music discovery because it offers remixes, live performances, and video integration through YouTube. Its recommendation system is also effective. However, the app feels cluttered due to mixed content like music and videos on the same screen. It also needs better navigation consistency, improved queue management, clearer error messages, and more controls in the mini player.

Overall, Spotify offers a smoother and more user-friendly experience, while YouTube Music provides better content variety but needs improvements in organization and usability.
